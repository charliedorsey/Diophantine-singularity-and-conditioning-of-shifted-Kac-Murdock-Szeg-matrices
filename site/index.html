The central result

A matrix problem becomes an arithmetic orbit.

This companion follows a fixed level τ\tau through the spectra of the shifted Kac–Murdock–Szegő matrices

TN(a)=[a|i−j|]i,j=0N−1. T_N(a)=[a^{|i-j|}]_{i,j=0}^{N-1}.
BN(a,τ)=TN(a)−τIN,0<a<1,N≥2. B_N(a,\tau)=T_N(a)-\tau I_N,\quad 0<a<1,\ N\ge2.
The central observation is exact: in the interior of the symbol band, singularity is controlled by a one-dimensional arithmetic residual,
detBN=0⇔Nα−β∈ℤ. \det B_N=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad N\alpha-\beta\in\mathbb Z.
This relation turns spectral crossings into Diophantine events and near crossings into condition-number growth.

The whole picture

Five positions, three mechanisms

The KMS symbol takes values between μ−=(1−a)/(1+a)\mu_-=(1-a)/(1+a) and μ+=(1+a)/(1−a)\mu_+=(1+a)/(1-a). For a fixed level τ\tau, the condition numbers have a bounded exterior regime, quadratic growth at either band edge, or Diophantine spikes in the interior.
